ABOUT BUFFALO MOUNTAIN DESIGN

Made in Alberta.
Shaped by distinct Indigenous roots.

Buffalo Mountain Design is an Indigenous-led jewelry and small-goods studio based in Alberta, Canada, creating beadwork and leather pieces with a contemporary, material-led approach.

OUR ROOTS

Identity informs the work, without becoming a shortcut for it.

The studio was founded by an Atikamekw/Woodland Cree maker. Beadwork sits at the centre of Buffalo Mountain Design: patient handwork, repetition, colour and pattern transformed into pieces meant to be worn and lived with.

The brand's marketing and storytelling are shaped by a wider Indigenous perspective informed by Tai-Dum heritage from northern Vietnam. These are distinct histories and experiences; they are not treated as interchangeable. Each brings its own relationship to community, place, material and visual storytelling.

01 · PLACE

Alberta is part of the visual language.

Mountain water, stone, prairie light, wildflowers and open landscape all inform how the work is photographed and presented. The jewelry is allowed to exist in conversation with the land rather than against a generic studio backdrop.

02 · MATERIAL

Beadwork first, with room to grow.

The current collection centres beaded jewelry, including necklaces and other wearable pieces. Leather work and small-scale objects will expand the material vocabulary of the studio over time.

03 · APPROACH

Contemporary design, made by hand.

Buffalo Mountain Design is interested in pieces that feel intentional, wearable and visually strong. The goal is not to flatten tradition into a style, but to make work that is personal, current and grounded in the people who create and share it.
